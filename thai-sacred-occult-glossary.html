Child-spirit figures and funerary charms

กุมารทอง

Kuman Thong

Golden child spirit

A child-spirit figure kept in a relationship of care, offerings, and expected assistance.

Hear Thai online ↗ See pictures ↗
Context & tradition

Literary and traditional accounts describe a fetus taken from the womb of a woman who died while pregnant, dried by fire, then lacquered and gilded. Contemporary devotional figures may instead be made from powders, herbs, or other materials. Keepers offer food, drinks, or toys and may treat the spirit as a child, believing it can protect the home or assist with good fortune.

Related: Prai Kuman · Luk Krok · Phi Tai Thang Klom

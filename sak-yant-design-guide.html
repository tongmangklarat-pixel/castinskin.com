Two introductory terms

Introductory term

Sacred tattooing

สักยันต์

Sak Yant

Tattooing sacred diagrams or characters within a tradition of belief and ritual.

Google Images · tattoo examples ↗Hear Thai online ↗
Traditional associations

Sak means tattooing; yant refers to the sacred design. The complete practice includes the inscription, its teacher and its ritual setting.

Glossary: สักยันต์ · Sak Yant ↗

Introductory term

Sacred diagram

ยันต์

Yant

A diagram whose lines, characters, numbers and other components convey meaning.

Google Images · tattoo examples ↗Hear Thai online ↗
Traditional associations

A yant can combine a geometric framework, sacred characters and symbolic images. These parts often appear together in the same tattoo.

Glossary: ยันต์ · Yant ↗

Three pillars of a design

The diagram, sacred characters and symbolic images are parts of a design. They overlap: inscriptions also appear around animals and sacred figures.

ยันต์

Yant · yantra

The diagram

Lines, grids, circles and spires give a design its structure. The word yant can also name the complete sacred design.

อักขระ

Akkhara

Sacred characters & formulas

Sacred letters, syllables and formulas appear throughout sak yant, inside diagrams and around figures. Khom is one script used for these inscriptions; it is not the name of a language.

รูปภาพ

Rup Phap

The images

Animals, flowers, deities and other figures provide a recognisable motif, often combined with diagrams and sacred writing.
